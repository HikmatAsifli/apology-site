Part III

Not promises. Actions.

I'm not going to promise you a hundred things again — I've already made too many promises I couldn't keep. So instead, here is what I will do:

01

Control my emotions

My frustration from trading — or anything else — will never again become your burden. My problems are mine to carry.

02

Separate my struggles from how I treat you

A bad day, a losing trade, a bad mood — none of it will ever be an excuse for coldness again.

03

Keep the promises I make

No more big words. Fewer promises, but ones I can actually stand behind.

04

Be present

Give you my time, my attention, and my patience — not the leftovers of my energy after everything else.

05

Stop making excuses

When I fail at something, I'll own it. No justification, no blame-shifting.

06

Work on myself first

You were never responsible for fixing me. That's my job — and I'm finally doing it.

"I don't want to tell you I've changed. I want you to see it for yourself."
